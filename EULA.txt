1. SOFTWARE DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------

EasyPi Mobile is the Android companion app for an EasyPi home server. It can
run an Android VPN service that filters DNS and network connections with the
home EasyPi's policy, carry the phone's traffic through an encrypted tunnel to
the home EasyPi or to a VPS, back up photos and videos, and show cameras.

It operates at the network level of the phone and may affect its connectivity.
